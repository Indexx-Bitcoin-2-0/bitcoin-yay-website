BTCY Revival Plan — Power Mining 2.0

Bitcoin Yay | October 19, 2026

I agree that Bitcoin Yay (BTCY) deserves a serious strategic review. If new-user registrations have fallen from approximately 1,000 per day to much lower levels, we need to examine not only marketing but also the product’s value proposition, mining economics, user confidence, and reasons people stop participating.

Your idea of allowing miners to purchase additional mining power is worth exploring. However, I would separate three different business models:

* Free Mining: Users earn BTCY Nuggets through participation, activity, and referrals.
* Power Mining: Users purchase additional computing or mining-related services to increase their eligible mining activity.
* Investment Products: Users contribute capital expecting financial returns, potentially 10%–15% annually.

The third model is fundamentally different from the first two. Calling an investment “Power Mining” or paying returns in BTCY Nuggets does not automatically exempt it from SEC securities laws.

I would not recommend designing the program to evade SEC oversight. Instead, we should explore a compliant mining-service product alongside a separately structured investment offering, if one makes commercial sense.

For example, a $10,000 purchase targeting 15% annually would require $1,500 in annual value, or $125 monthly on a simple-interest basis.

But the important question is: Where does the $1,500 come from?

If it comes from sustainable, independently verified mining revenue or another legitimate business activity, we can evaluate the economics. If it comes primarily from selling new mining packages to new users, that creates substantial financial and regulatory risk.

We also need to distinguish BTCY Nuggets from transferable BTCY tokens. Their conversion eligibility, actual liquidity, and realizable market value determine whether they have meaningful economic value.

2. My proposed BTCY revival strategy

Rebuild user acquisition

Relaunch referral campaigns, short-form videos, and creator partnerships. Focus on verified registrations and miners who remain active after 30 days, not just sign-ups.

Introduce Power Mining 2.0

Test $5,000, $10,000, and $20,000 packages only after establishing what mining services are actually delivered, how rewards are generated, and whether the pricing is commercially reasonable.

Build trust and transparency

Publish clear explanations of Nugget balances, conversion rules, withdrawal eligibility, costs, and risks. Avoid advertising guaranteed interest without a legally authorized and financially supported product.

Measure retention and revenue

Track daily newcomers, active miners, mining-package conversions, customer acquisition costs, withdrawals, and the economic sustainability of rewards.
