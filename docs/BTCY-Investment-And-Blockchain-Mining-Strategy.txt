BTCY Blockchain Mining & Investment Compliance Strategy

My recommendation is to explore converting BTCY Power Mining into a blockchain-verifiable mining and rewards system, while keeping any investment offering legally separate.

Blockchain technology could improve transparency, prove how rewards are calculated, and support customer trust. But putting BTCY Nuggets on a blockchain does not automatically make the program SEC-compliant.

For Bitcoin Yay, I would evaluate three models:

Model	Technology	Regulatory considerations
A. Real blockchain mining	Actual proof-of-work computing, verified mining output	Potentially more favorable securities analysis for certain mining activities
B. Blockchain reward system	On-chain BTCY tokens backed by transparent off-chain activity records	Token issuance, investment-contract and consumer protection analysis
C. Regulated investment	Blockchain records plus legally structured investment contracts	Securities registration or exemption, investor verification, disclosures

I would prioritize Model B for the existing BTCY mobile application, because it may be closer to how your Nugget rewards currently operate, and investigate Model A as a separate, genuine mining business.

Model C would be appropriate only if BTCY wants to raise customer capital in exchange for anticipated financial returns.

1. What the SEC actually permits

The SEC issued an important crypto-asset interpretation effective March 23, 2026, covering certain crypto activities, including proof-of-work mining.

The distinction is important:

* Actual protocol mining: Miners contribute computational resources to a public blockchain, helping validate transactions and earning protocol rewards.
* BTCY app mining: If the application merely credits Nuggets according to an internal formula, that does not necessarily constitute protocol mining.
* Investment mining: If BTCY sells packages based on expected profits from BTCY’s business efforts, the arrangement may constitute an investment contract.

The SEC’s guidance provides a more favorable framework for certain genuine protocol mining activities, but not a blanket exemption for cloud mining contracts, token sales, or guaranteed-yield programs.

3. Technology and licensing options

Technology or service	Purpose
Solana	BTCY token issuance and transparent transfer records
Chainlink	Potentially publish independently sourced data used for reward calculations
Fireblocks	Institutional wallet and digital-asset custody infrastructure
Sumsub or similar	Identity verification, KYC and AML workflow support
Actual mining pool integration	Verify real hashrate, mining activity, payouts and operating costs

These technologies can support compliance operations. None grants an SEC exemption, money transmitter license, or approval to promise investment returns.

FinCEN has previously distinguished genuine computer rental services for mining from virtual-currency administration and money transmission. Whether that distinction applies to BTCY would depend on its actual operations.

4. How I would organize BTCY

My proposed structure would have two business divisions:

Division A — BTCY Mining & Rewards

Maintain the free BTCY app, Nuggets, referrals, and optional paid services. If real mining is added, establish measurable computing capacity, pool payouts, customer entitlements, and transparent fees.

Division B — BTCY Investment Offering

If BTCY wants to accept $5,000–$20,000 with expected financial returns, consider a separate securities offering under an appropriate registration exemption.

For example, Regulation D Rule 506(c) permits public advertising if every purchaser is an accredited investor and verification requirements are satisfied. Regulation Crowdfunding may be another pathway for eligible retail investors through a registered intermediary.

The two divisions would require separate contracts, disclosures, accounting, and marketing controls. Separation alone does not eliminate securities obligations.

6. My most important recommendation

Do not spend significant development money building a new blockchain just to make Power Mining appear more compliant.

BTCY may be able to use its existing token infrastructure, together with better reward verification, reporting, and a genuinely delivered service.

The biggest legal question remains whether the customer is purchasing a service or investing money in expectation of BTCY-generated profits. The SEC explicitly considers promises about managerial efforts when applying the investment-contract test.

I need to confirm one technical fact before recommending the final architecture:

How does BTCY currently generate Nuggets?

BTCY’s own software generates Nuggets based on time, activity and purchased mining power.

BTCY operates actual blockchain mining computers or contracts with mining pools.

BTCY combines internally generated Nuggets with actual cryptocurrency mining revenue.

Develop BTCY compliance architecture

Once we establish that fact, we can design the blockchain technology around BTCY’s actual business rather than building a system that still carries the same regulatory risk.
